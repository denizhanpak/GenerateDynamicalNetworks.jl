GenerateDynamicalNetworks.cma_search — Method
cma_search(build, spec, θ0, σ0, lower, upper; budget, restarts=0, popsize=8, runs=16, T=1000, dtscale=0.04,
           seed=1, base=1_000_000, maxtime=nothing) -> (; θ, candidates, evals, log)

CMA-ES (CMAEvolutionStrategy.jl) on θ ↦ score(simulate(build(θ))) within the box [lower, upper], restarting with twice the population while budget evaluations last. Each restart's population mean is a candidate; with several, the one scoring best on four times the runs, on unused seeds, wins. A finite maxtime stops each CMA-ES run after that many seconds, so the result then depends on the machine's speed.

GenerateDynamicalNetworks.corner_path — Method

A shortest corner path from s to t through unused corners, neighbours tried in random order. The last step may land on t from s directly only if t → s isn't already a step (a cube edge can carry one direction only, so a two-way pair needs two separate paths).

source
GenerateDynamicalNetworks.corner_rates — Method

Corner rates from a layout. Along a path each step u → w leaves u at its out-rate (out[e] for a state, fast for a transit) and arrives at w along a contracting direction, −contract. Every other direction at a used corner contracts at contract. Unused corners point toward the network, by distance to it.

GenerateDynamicalNetworks.correct_edges — Method
correct_edges(build, θ0, spec, edges, lower, upper; passes, runs, T, dtscale=0.01, base=100_000) -> (trace, θ)

Correction passes on θ = [log speed; log edge rates; …]: every edge out of state i moves by log(measured dwell / τᵢ), and the edges of a branch also by the branch error divided by L (the sensitivity from the low-noise scaling). Returns one row per pass and the θ after the last update, clamped to [lower, upper].

source
GenerateDynamicalNetworks.correct_rates — Method
correct_rates(spec, build, out0; passes=12, base=100_000, response=nothing, cap=Inf, T=250Στ,
              checkpoint=nothing, deadline=Inf, verbose=false) -> (; out, trace)

Correction passes on out-rates (one per edge, in edge_list order) for a model build(out). Each pass simulates the model and takes, per state, one Broyden step on x = asinh.(λₑ/c) over its out-edges against y = [−log dwell; log branch ratios], with c 0.05× the smallest starting |rate|. asinh behaves like log for large rates and is linear through zero, so a rate may turn negative (the excitable side). The Jacobian starts from response(o, out, c) (or initial_response) and is learned between passes, skipping states with a branch that saw no visits. Passes use 32 runs at dtscale = 0.02; the last 3 use 96 runs at 0.01 and steps of at most 0.3 in x. cap bounds every rate's size on both sides. The last update is returned, with a trace of every pass.

Runs go in chunks of 16. With checkpoint (a file path) the state is saved after every chunk and a later call resumes from it. With deadline (a time() value) it throws OutOfTime rather than start a chunk expected to end after it.

GenerateDynamicalNetworks.ctrnn_correct_offsets — Method
ctrnn_correct_offsets(spec, layout, d0; by_state=true, passes, late_runs, base=100_000,
                      checkpoint=nothing, deadline=Inf, verbose=false) -> d

Correction passes on the edge offsets; returns the offsets after the last update. Each pass simulates the current design on fresh seeds (32 runs of length 250Στ; the last three passes use late_runs runs at half the step).

  • by_state=true (10 passes, late_runs=192): one Broyden step per state. Inputs are the log of the state's total 1-D exit rate and the log rate ratios of its edges; outputs the measured log exit rate (1/dwell) and log branch ratios. The Jacobian starts at the identity and is learned from pass to pass.
  • by_state=false (8 passes, late_runs=96): each edge's passage time moves by its own measured rate error, at most a factor e per pass.

With checkpoint (a file path) the state is saved after every chunk of 16 runs and a later call resumes from it. With deadline (a time() value) it throws OutOfTime rather than start a chunk it expects to end later, judging from the last chunk of the same call.

GenerateDynamicalNetworks.ctrnn_passage_time — Method
ctrnn_passage_time(d, A, g, τ, ε; n=6000)

Mean time for one cell, resting at −d with on level A, sharpness g, time constant τ and noise ε, to reach A/2: the 1-D first-passage integral on a grid of n points. It is evaluated in plain floating point, which is fine at the design's levels (A = 12 noise SDs) but can overflow to NaN for much higher barriers (it did for one test cell with A at 38 noise SDs).

GenerateDynamicalNetworks.dwell_rates — Method
dwell_rates(form, τ, ε_eff, β; κ=0.1, νmin=2.0) -> (α, ν)

One shared rate α and a saddle value ν per state, so each state's designed dwell is τ[i]. α is the largest needed with ν at νmin; then each ν is solved by root finding. Throws an ArgumentError for a dwell longer than the noise allows.

GenerateDynamicalNetworks.edge_rate_fit — Method
edge_rate_fit(build, spec, θ0, lower, upper; passes, runs, T, budget, popsize, seconds, label="", verbose=false) -> θ

The fit the simplex and cylinder backends share (the prototype's phase 4 fit_backend), on θ = [log speed; log edge rates; …] in the box [lower, upper]: passes correction passes (correct_edges) from θ0. If the best of them passes a check on 32 held-out runs it is the result; otherwise a CMA-ES search from it of at most budget evaluations or seconds seconds, then two correction passes of 32 runs of length 4T.

GenerateDynamicalNetworks.edge_seed_rates — Method
edge_seed_rates(spec, edges) -> (; α, logs, seeded)

A shared speed α and a log rate per edge. Speeds and per-state rates come from the Horchler dwell design with one noise value (dwell_rates in the u form); branches split by the low-noise scaling, a rate ratio of about p^(1/L) with L = max(log(√0.1/ε), 1). seeded is false when the design has no solution, in which case a rough guess from the dwells is used instead.

GenerateDynamicalNetworks.hop_design — Method
hop_design(L, out; fast, contract, margin=contract/4, soft=10, ridge=1e-6) -> (; J, b, k, worst)

Neuron rows from out-rates: for each neuron j, kj from its slowest departure, then (bj, J_j·) by least squares on the used vertices' rates subject to the departures (equalities) and to every other used vertex's rate along j being ≤ −margin. Unused vertices next to the network are asked, softly, to leave toward it at ≥ margin. worst is the largest relative violation of the hard conditions.

Solved by OSQP to 1e-9 with a fixed adaptive_rho_interval = 25: OSQP's default sets that interval from measured setup time, which made the design differ from run to run (by up to 7e-10 relative).

source
GenerateDynamicalNetworks.hop_layout — Method
hop_layout(spec; Msearch=7, tries=3000, keep=40, seed=1, tol=1e-3) -> SyncLayout

The synchrony backend's search for the fewest neurons, keeping only layouts whose every neuron's design is met (worst violation below tol) at the rule-of-thumb rates, cheapest first, up to Msearch neurons; if none is met, the one-hot layout with relay neurons. Random layouts of a bigger cube get less feasible, not more (pairwise flips must be threshold functions of the other neurons), so the search stops early and the structured layout adds the neurons.

GenerateDynamicalNetworks.horchler_design — Method
horchler_design(P, τ; form=:u, noise, κ=0.1, νmin=2.0, hidden=true) -> NamedTuple

The closed-form recipe: repair, branch noise (each two-way branch's odds set by the ratio of its targets' noise), effective noise per state (effective_noise), the shared rate α and saddle values ν (dwell_rates), then ρ. τ is the dwell to request, which a correction pass may move away from the target. Throws an ArgumentError if the graph can't be repaired or a dwell is longer than the noise allows.

GenerateDynamicalNetworks.initial_response — Function
initial_response(k, law=log(10))

A state's starting Broyden matrix over its k out-edges, from inputs asinh(λₑ/c) to outputs [−log dwell; log share ratios]: a faster exit along any edge shortens the dwell and shifts the share toward that edge. The share response scales with law = ln(1/ε): at low noise the larger rate wins almost every time, so shares are far steeper in the rate ratio.

GenerateDynamicalNetworks.race — Method
race(λs, contract, η; n=241, dt0=1e-4, grow=1.002, tail=1e-9) -> (dwell, shares)

Mean dwell and branch shares of independent racing exits with departure rates λs and contraction contract (one value, or one per exit), at noise η. Each exit's survival from ψ = 0 solves the backward equation by implicit Euler on a time grid that starts at dt0 and grows by grow per step, until the joint survival falls below tail; the integrals are trapezoid sums on the same grid.

GenerateDynamicalNetworks.simplex_model — Method
simplex_model(spec, θ) -> LVModel

dxⱼ = s·xⱼ(1 − |x|² + Σᵢ aᵢⱼxᵢ²) dt + ε dW as a u-form LVModel, with θ = [log s; log aᵢⱼ for each edge in edge_list order; log cᵢ for each state]: aᵢⱼ is the edge's rate on an edge i → j, −cᵢ off the graph and 0 on the diagonal. The noise ε is the specification's.

GenerateDynamicalNetworks.stone_holmes_mean — Method
stone_holmes_mean(δ, ε, λu, λs)

Leading-order mean passage time past a saddle with unstable and stable eigenvalues λu and λs, neighbourhood size δ and noise ε ≪ δ (Stone & Holmes 1990): (2(log δ − log ε) + log 4λu − log1p(λu/λs) + γ) / 2λu, with γ Euler's constant. This is passagetime from SHCTools.jl.

GenerateDynamicalNetworks.subdivide — Method
subdivide(P, τ, a, b) -> (P, τ)
