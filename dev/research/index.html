Research record

The package was built from a research prototype, developed in phases. Each phase has a notebook that records what was tried and what the runs showed. They are kept as a record: the code and numbers in them are the prototype's, not the package's, and they aren't updated.

PhaseTopicNotebook
0Simulator and optimiser library benchmarksphase 0
1Core layer: specification checks, graph repair, dwell measurementphase 1
2Horchler Lotka–Volterra designphase 2
3Fitting by search and correctionphase 3
4Simplex and cylinder constructionsphase 4

The pages are static: each shows the notebook's output as it was when exported, and its controls don't respond. They load Pluto's scripts and styles from the web.

The notebooks themselves, including those of the later phases (classification and the two-population and CTRNN backends, synchrony, Hopfield), are Pluto files in the repository's research/notebooks/ folder, with the prototype code and stored results beside them in research/benchmarks/.
